# Portfolio goals

The Scout reads this every week. Edit it whenever priorities change.

## What this site is for
Sreeram Ganesan's personal portfolio: a credible, memorable showcase of a Senior Full Stack Engineer
(15+ years across Retail, Healthcare and AI) growing toward Principal Engineer, and a place where a visitor
learns something worthwhile within a minute of landing.

## Who visits
1. Engineers, engineering leaders and hiring managers judging depth and judgement.
2. Curious learners who arrive from search or a link and want something explained clearly.
3. Peers working on generative AI and agents.

The owner is not currently marked as available for work (`SITE.available` is false in `lib/data.ts`).
Optimise for credibility and learning value, not for job-application conversion.

## What "stronger" means
- **Educational first.** Visitors should leave knowing something they did not know before.
- **Credible.** Real craft on show: performance, accessibility, clean interaction. No buzzword padding.
- **Fresh.** The site reflects what is genuinely new in web, AI and agents, not last year's stack.
- **Fast and comfortable on a phone.** Most visits are on mobile.

## Content direction (educational)
Formats to grow over time, in the spirit of the existing Lab pages (How the Web Works, AI Lab, Performance
Monitor, Performance Glossary):
- **Tech Radar**: technologies marked Adopt, Trial or Watch, each with a one-line reason and a source.
- **Did you know?**: short, surprising, true facts about the web, AI and engineering, each with a source and date.
- **Explainers**: short animated or interactive pages that make one idea click from first principles.
- **What changed**: small "as of <date>" notes on meaningful platform or AI developments.

Topic areas: the web platform and performance; generative AI and multi-agent systems (Claude, MCP, agent
frameworks); retail and healthcare technology (commerce, booking, interoperability); engineering practice
and leadership.

Never: hype, rumours, funding news, benchmark-of-the-week claims, unsourced statistics.

## Hard rules (on top of CLAUDE.md)
- Never invent facts, opinions, metrics or experience for Sreeram. Anything personal comes from `lib/data.ts`
  or from a reply by the owner. If a proposal needs one, ask for it (`needs_owner_input`).
- Every educational fact carries a primary source URL and an as-of date in its data entry.
- Blue palette only. No personal contact details; LinkedIn and GitHub only.
- The daily "latest tech" briefing is delivered to the owner by email through a separate crew. Do not put a
  briefing in the proposals issue and do not propose a live news feed.
